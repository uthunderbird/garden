# Garden

## Purpose
Curated digital garden for public-facing writing and structured presentation of selected personal materials.

## Scope
In scope:
- curated content intended for publication or public browsing
- topic sections and information architecture
- bilingual or public-facing entry pages

Out of scope:
- raw writing backlog
- broad editorial ideation
- private planning material
